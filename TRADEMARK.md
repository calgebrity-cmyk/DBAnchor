# DBAnchor Trademark & Brand Policy

This document outlines the policy and guidelines for the use of the **DBAnchor** name, logos, and related brand assets.

---

## 1. Purpose of This Policy

The goal of this policy is to encourage open-source community adoption, extensions, and ecosystem development while protecting the identity, integrity, and trust associated with the **DBAnchor** project.

While the source code of DBAnchor is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**, that license explicitly does **not** grant trademark rights:

> *"This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the Work..."* — Apache License 2.0, Section 6.

This Trademark Policy defines clear, developer-friendly guidelines for how the names and marks may be used.

---

## 2. Our Trademarks

The marks covered by this policy include:
- **DBAnchor** / **dbanchor** (primary word mark)
- **dbx** (short alias CLI command used within the DBAnchor project context; note that `dbx` is a common, generic CLI abbreviation and this mark applies only when directly associated with the DBAnchor project)
- DBAnchor official logos, icons, and badges

> **Note**: The description *"database middleware and diagnostics"* is a general functional description of what the software does, not a proprietary tagline claimed as a trademark. Anyone may accurately use similar descriptive language when explaining what DBAnchor is or what their own software does.

---

## 3. Allowed / Free Use (No Permission Needed)

You may freely use the DBAnchor name and marks without explicit written permission in the following cases:

### A. Accurate Technical Descriptions & Nominative Use
- Stating truthfully that your software, library, or application connects to, integrates with, or is built using DBAnchor.
- Examples of permitted titles/descriptions:
  - *"FastAPI DBAnchor Plugin"*
  - *"Supabase Connector for DBAnchor"*
  - *"Powered by DBAnchor"*
  - *"Compatible with DBAnchor v0.1+"*

### B. Educational & Community Content
- Articles, blog posts, books, tutorials, conference presentations, meetups, podcasts, and academic research discussing DBAnchor.
- Sharing community benchmark results, diagnostic examples, or workflow guides.

### C. Community Package Naming
When building third-party plugins or extensions, please use naming conventions that make it clear the package is a community extension rather than an official core project release:
- **Preferred**: `dbanchor-ext-<name>`, `dbanchor-plugin-<name>`, `<name>-for-dbanchor`
- **Avoid**: `dbanchor-<core-feature>` in a way that suggests it is the official upstream release.

---

## 4. Forks & Derivative Works

We welcome open-source forks. Forking is a healthy part of open-source software, and you may fork and modify DBAnchor under the terms of the Apache 2.0 license.

When distributing a publicly released fork, we ask that:
- You clearly indicate in your repository description, README, and package metadata that the project is a fork or derivative of DBAnchor, not the official upstream release (e.g. *"Based on DBAnchor"*, *"A fork of DBAnchor"*).
- If your fork introduces significant architectural differences that may affect stability or compatibility, consider renaming the core package or binary to avoid confusion for end users.

There is no requirement to rename for private use, internal tooling, or experimental forks that are not publicly distributed.

---

## 5. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use the DBAnchor marks in ways that cause confusion, imply false sponsorship, or misrepresent the origin of the software:

1. **No False Endorsement**: You may not use the marks in a manner that suggests DBAnchor or its maintainers endorse, sponsor, or certify your product, service, or commercial offering without prior written agreement.
2. **No Confusingly Similar Names**: You may not register domain names, social media handles, companies, or trademarks that contain "DBAnchor" or confusingly similar variants (e.g. `dbanchor-cloud.com`, `official-dbanchor.org`).
3. **No Misleading Commercial SaaS**: You may not offer a commercial database service labeled simply as "DBAnchor Official Cloud" or otherwise implying it is the canonical upstream product.

## 6. Logo & Visual Guidelines

The official DBAnchor logo is located at [`assets/logos/dbanchor-logo.png`](assets/logos/dbanchor-logo.png) in the repository.

When using official DBAnchor logos and badges:
- Do not distort, stretch, recolor, or modify the logo proportions.
- Maintain adequate clear space around the mark.
- Do not combine the logo with other company logos in a way that creates a single merged trademark.
